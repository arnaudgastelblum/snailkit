# PDF export

![PDF export](../assets/scenes/pdf-export.webp)

Take your note's look with you: your theme, images and diagrams in a PDF with selectable text and clickable links.

## Try it

1. Right-click a note and choose **Snailkit: Export to PDF**.
2. Choose where to save. The PDF opens in your reader.

## Good to know

- Needs the desktop app and loads the web images your note already shows when printing.
- Put `%% pagebreak %%` alone on a line to start a new page.
- Try landscape for wide tables and diagrams.
