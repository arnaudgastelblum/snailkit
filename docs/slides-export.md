# Slides to PowerPoint

![Slides to PowerPoint](../assets/scenes/slides-export.webp)

Turn your Excalidraw frames into a PowerPoint presentation. Each frame becomes a picture on its own slide.

## Try it

1. Open an Excalidraw drawing and draw a frame around each slide.
2. Run **Snailkit: Export slideshow to PowerPoint (.pptx)** from the command palette.

## Good to know

- Needs the **Excalidraw** plugin, enabled, version **2.27.0 or newer**.
- Use Excalidraw's **Slideshow** side panel to order frames or leave some out.
- On mobile, the presentation saves in your vault.
- Slides are pictures; their text and shapes cannot be edited in PowerPoint.
