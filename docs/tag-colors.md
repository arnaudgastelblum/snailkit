# Tags

![Tags](../assets/scenes/tag-colors.webp)

Recognize your tags at a glance. Each family gets a steady color, with its sub-tags joined beside it.

## Try it

1. Turn on **Tags** in **Settings → Snailkit**.
2. Open a note with tags and move the cursor away from one to see its capsule.
3. Right-click a tag to choose **Change tag color…** or **Change family color…**.

## Good to know

- Coloring leaves the text in your notes unchanged.
- Colors follow your light or dark theme.
- Enable **Tag placeholder on tasks** to choose a tag from a small button beside untagged tasks.
