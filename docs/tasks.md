# Tasks

![Tasks](../assets/scenes/tasks.webp)

Write a task where it comes up, and find it again in one place. You never have to choose between
the two.

## The idea

- **Write them anywhere.** In a meeting note, a project, today's daily note: `- [ ] Send the quote #client`.
  The tag says what it belongs to. The **Tags** tool suggests it as you type.
- **Find them all.** The **Tasks** tab of the Workbench gathers every tagged task of your vault in
  one list, grouped by tag, with **Today** and **Upcoming** views.
- **Handle them from there.** Check, prioritize, give a date, rename or move a task to another
  tag: the note is updated for you. Add one in a few keystrokes: `Call the bank !1 @tomorrow`.
- **Develop them.** Each task can have its own note: write in the **Note** area of its details
  and the note is created, linked from the task line with a small 📝. Long texts, images, links:
  open it as a normal note with the arrow.
- **Break them into steps.** The checkboxes you write in a task's note are its steps: in the list
  they show right under the task (click its `1/4` count to fold them), and a new one is offered the
  task's tag in one click.

## Good to know

- On a phone, Tasks opens on **Today**, laid out as on a computer (Your day, Worth a look with its
  buttons on every row, From your brainstorms, Waiting since earlier), and tags fold by weight in
  the same way. One line of buttons filters the list (Tag, Priority, Type, search); a tap unfolds
  its choices, a second tap folds them, and a tag picked there opens as a room with its color. A
  tap on the round + button unfolds your most used tags (a tag with sub-tags opens them); tap one
  and type the task. In a tag's room, the + adds straight into that tag. What you add lands right
  under the field.
- Open the list from the ribbon, the Note menu or Home. Right click a task for every action.
- **Weight you can see.** The list is sorted by priority: a high task is a little larger, a low one
  smaller and grey, and `1` `2` `3` make the task glide to its new place. In **All tasks**, each tag
  shows what weighs and folds the rest under one line (**12 lighter · 2 waiting**); on a tag's own
  page everything shows. What you checked today stays at the foot of its tag, struck through, until
  midnight.
- **Tags in the navigator** show their whole capsule (a sub-tag reads `PROJECT website`). Right-click
  one to move it, rename it in the vault, or, with Tags on, change its color (or its family's).
- **A tag is a place.** Pick a tag in the navigator: the head takes its color, a path leads back,
  and the right column shows the day for that tag, the notes its tasks are written in and its
  sub-tags (until you pick a task).
- **New task** sits on top of the list. Hover it (or press `N`) and your most used tags unfold as a
  small map, their sub-tags one step further: click one and type the task (`Enter` adds it and keeps
  the line for the next one). Under each tag, **+ New task** adds right there. Type `!1` for the
  priority, `@fri` or `@tomorrow` for the date, `#tag` for another tag: what it becomes shows under
  the line before you press `Enter`.
- **Flags** say how a task is done: `#quick` and `#deep` sit next to **Everything** over the list
  (**10 minutes**, **Focus**) to pick what fits your time and energy; `#waiting` and `#someday` leave Today and get
  their own entry. Declare them under **Flag tags** (one click adds the suggested ones).
- Checking is a small pleasure: the task flies into **done today**, which rolls one up, the ring of
  the day fills, and the sound climbs while you keep going. A clear day gets a little scene. Turn
  it off with **Playful checking** (off by itself when your system asks for less motion).
- Keyboard: `↑` `↓` to move, `X` to check, `T` for today, `N` for a new task.
- Several at once: `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+click or `Shift`+click (or `Shift`+`↑` `↓`, `Ctrl`+`A`), then
  check, date, prioritize, tag or delete them together, or drag them onto a tag. One Undo for all.
- A checkbox written without a tag is not lost: if its note changed in the last 30 days, it waits
  under **No tag** at the bottom of the list, with a **tag** button to give it one.
- Choosing a tag (the **tag** button, a Brainstorm, this list) starts with what is used **around
  the note**: the tags of its tasks, then those of the notes above it, with where they were found.
  Below come **your tags**, as the navigator shows them. Other tags of the vault come up as you type.
- **Today** starts with **Your day** (what is due today, the heaviest first, and what you checked
  today). Then **Worth a look**, the morning triage: tasks due within 7 days, **High** ones without
  a date, and tasks new these days (first seen in the last 3 days) without a weight. Pick one and
  decide: `T` today, `1` `2` `3` its weight, `→` **Later** (out of this list for 3 days); the next
  one is picked for you. On the right, the **Week ahead** counts what is due today, tomorrow, the
  day after and the rest of the week. When Snailkit first saw a task is remembered in its settings
  (a task line has no creation date); the tasks already there the first time count as old.
  With Brainstorm on, **From your brainstorms** (folded) offers the sentences of your brainstorms in
  progress that look like tasks, with a tag (the brainstorm's context, else one learned or used
  around the note; click it to change it): **Make it a task** writes it as a task in its
  brainstorm, as Sort does; **Not a task** keeps it as an idea, never offered again.
  Those from earlier days wait quietly below, folded, with
  **All to today**, **All to tomorrow** or **Remove dates** to settle them in one go (one Undo
  for all). A late task reads **since Oct 7**, in grey. Prefer the overdue ones first, in red?
  **Show overdue first** in the settings. Sorting and the priorities shown are under **⋯**.
- Nothing is stored apart: everything is plain Markdown in your notes, readable by other tools.
