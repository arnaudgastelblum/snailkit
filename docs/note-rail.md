# Note menu

![Note menu](../assets/scenes/note-rail.webp)

Keep your note's contents, pins, open tasks and daily calendar within reach. A small row of icons follows you from note to note.

## Try it

1. Turn on **Note menu** in **Settings → Snailkit** and open a note.
2. Hover an icon to open its panel, or tap it on a phone.
3. Click the pin at the top of a panel to keep it open as you change notes.

## Good to know

- Hover a heading to peek at that section; click to stay there.
- Pin notes to the current note or to the whole vault.
- Click a calendar day to open its daily note or create it from your template.
