# Note menu

![Note menu](../assets/scenes/note-rail.webp)

A small row of icons beside every note. At the top, the snail opens the Snailkit menu; below the rule, everything is about the note you are reading.

## Try it

1. Turn on **Note menu** in **Settings → Snailkit** and open a note.
2. Hover an icon to open its panel, or tap it on a phone. Click the pin at the top of a panel to keep it open as you change notes.
3. Hover the **snail**: Home, Tasks, Brainstorms, Search, today's note, the Calendar and the settings, each there while its tool is on.
4. Hover the **colored initial**: the way up to the top of your tree and the notes under this one. Click a row to go there.
5. Open **Actions** (the sparkles, at the bottom): insert a table, a callout or a drawing while editing; export to PDF or move a selection to another note with those tools on.

## Good to know

- Contents: hover a heading to peek at that section; click to stay there.
- Open tasks: the unchecked tasks of the note, and what is due in the vault with the Tasks tool.
- Bookmarks: pin notes to the current note or to the whole vault.
- Calendar: click a day to open its daily note or create it from your template.
- The rail stands next to the text; a panel opens over it and never moves it.
